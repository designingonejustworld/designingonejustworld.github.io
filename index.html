Brief a new carousel

The 8-line template

  1. Topic / hook — one line
  2. Bucket — Changemaker Stories · Proof of Impact · Knowledge Tools · Culture & Aspiration · Institutional Shift · Seasonal Myth Busting
  3. Frame(s) — at least one of: Wellness, Sustainability, Economically Smart, Compassion, Youth Leadership, Future-Focused India
  4. Audience — Gen Z changemakers / Institutional / Mix
  5. Slide count — usually 5–8
  6. CTA — Soft / Hard / Institutional / None
  7. Photos — uploading? or leave Canva placeholders? or text-only?
  8. Canvas — IG/LI portrait 1080×1350 (default) · LI square 1200×1200 · Story 1080×1920
